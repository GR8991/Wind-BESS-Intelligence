# Patent-oriented technical workstream

This folder is for invention discovery and evidence, not a legal conclusion.

## Required records
- Technical problem and limitations of conventional methods
- Exact algorithm/method claimed to be different
- Versioned equations, assumptions, and flow diagrams
- Prior-art search log: query, database, date, publication/patent ID, relevant passage, distinction
- Baseline definition and reproducible test data
- Experimental protocol, results, failures, and negative results
- Version history and contributor/inventor records
- Confidentiality and public-disclosure log

## Candidate experiments
1. Compare sizing results against a transparent moving-average/ramp baseline.
2. Use synthetic profiles with known ramps, flat power, step changes, curtailment, and multi-hour deficits.
3. Sweep sampling interval, missing-data rate, wind variability, efficiency, SOC window, and POI limits.
4. Compare requested target against delivered output; report residual energy and violations.
5. Test out-of-sample days and multiple seasons.
6. Quantify any claimed improvement with a predeclared metric; do not select metrics after seeing results.

## Prior-art sources to search
- WIPO PATENTSCOPE
- Google Patents
- Espacenet
- Indian Patent Advanced Search System (InPASS)
- IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, NREL publications, and relevant grid-code/utility studies

Search concepts such as wind-storage sizing, battery sizing from renewable time series, ramp-rate control, firming, storage dispatch, SOC-constrained optimization, and uncertainty-aware sizing. Have a registered patent professional assess claim scope and applicable law before filing or public disclosure.
